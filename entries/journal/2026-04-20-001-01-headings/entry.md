# Level one

<!-- block: 01KPMM1WK2D97DQ7E6WT8X6VCJ -->
This is the canonical page title. Every entry has exactly one of these and it doubles as the document title in the browser tab.

## Level two

<!-- block: 01KPMM1WK2S3PJG4WV4AAB3ZGV -->
Used for major sections. This is what most long-form content actually needs most of the time.

<!-- block: 01KPMM1WK2HQWQSD45VN0N66B3 -->
### Level three

<!-- block: 01KPMM1WK211HTNCB3E332GRC8 -->
For subdivisions within a level-two section. A typical tutorial lives at level three.

<!-- block: 01KPMM1WK2BQYZ9EGG5X9VFN0E -->
#### Level four

<!-- block: 01KPMM1WK2N2KKE6AKH6HZAGYN -->
Rare. When you reach this depth you should probably be splitting the entry.

##### Level five
