# Lists

<!-- block: 01KPMM1XA4711Q4YBS0K6DZEFQ -->
## Unordered

<!-- block: 01KPMM1XA4BNZY3TA34D9W9DYF -->
- First item. Dash-prefixed.
- Second item. Can be as long as a paragraph if you want.
- Third item.

<!-- block: 01KPMM1XA437BKXYQT2KSA07D4 -->
## Ordered

<!-- block: 01KPMM1XA42A3KATG6N1V8MABQ -->
1. Step one. Numbered.
2. Step two. The numbers restart from 1 in every list automatically.
3. Step three.

<!-- block: 01KPMM1XA4N01N9GJKF6HMZ8FQ -->
## Nested

<!-- block: 01KPMM1XA4PKWQ0A6512RHRRSY -->
- Top-level bullet.
  - Nested under it. Indentation is what signals nesting.
  - Another nested sibling.
    - A third level. Rare in practice.
- Back to the top level after nesting.

<!-- block: 01KPMM1XA4A0GW4R9H8GCWNQ3F -->
## Mixed

<!-- block: 01KPMM1XA43E99Y60FT896QRSQ -->
1. First ordered step.
   - With an unordered sub-bullet that explains it.
   - And another.
2. Second ordered step.
