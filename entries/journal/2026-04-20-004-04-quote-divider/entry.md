# Quotes and dividers

<!-- block: 01KPMM1XKN4Y5X46MQHTZB2G28 -->
A blockquote cites someone else's words or flags a passage as meta. The Foghorn renderer gives it a subtle left border and a slight font shift so the reader's eye picks out the change.

<!-- block: 01KPMM1XKNWGP780FNQWWJR8XF -->
> The best way to predict the future is to invent it.
>
> — Alan Kay

<!-- block: 01KPMM1XKNAQB1Q9G2JZJ9V6BB -->
Quotes can span multiple paragraphs and can themselves contain **bold** or *italic* marks.

<!-- block: 01KPMM1XKNRC1YVGDYV7Z0XY68 -->
---

<!-- block: 01KPMM1XKNXJ5J65Z4565GZ49P -->
A thematic break is the horizontal line above this paragraph. It's what you reach for when you want a scene change without a heading — a softer "next section" signal than an H2. Useful inside long entries where the topic shifts but doesn't deserve a new heading of its own.

<!-- block: 01KPMM1XKNETNYQ2E44NME27K4 -->
After a thematic break the narration naturally pauses, which we use to our advantage in audio-first entries.
